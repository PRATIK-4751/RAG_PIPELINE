Advanced RAG Techniques

RAG systems have evolved beyond basic retrieval. Modern approaches include:

1. Query rewriting - The user query is expanded or reformulated before retrieval to improve recall.
2. Re-ranking - Retrieved chunks are re-scored using a cross-encoder for better precision.
3. Multi-hop retrieval - Complex questions are decomposed into sub-questions, each retrieved separately.
4. RAG with citations - The model generates answers with inline citations linking to specific source chunks.

Benefits of advanced RAG:
- Higher accuracy on complex questions
- Better handling of multi-document queries
- Reduced hallucination through structured citation
- Improved user trust through transparency

Challenges:
- Increased latency from multi-step retrieval
- More complex evaluation needed
- Computational cost of re-ranking
